Privacy policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal information.

Last updated 10 October 2026

Midsomer Design (“we”, “us”) designs, builds and looks after websites for small businesses. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, who else handles it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

When you send us an enquiry through the form on this website, we collect the details you give us: your name and email address, and if you choose to include them, your phone number, your current website and anything you tell us about your business.

We also use Google Analytics to see how many people visit this website and which pages they look at. It uses cookies to count visits, and the reports we see don’t identify you.

We don’t take payments on this website, and we don’t use advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to your enquiry, arrange a call and, if you go ahead, provide our services to you. We don’t sell your personal information.

Who else handles it

This website relies on a few service providers, who handle information on our behalf:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Cloudflare runs the service that receives the enquiry form and passes it on to us.
  • Google Analytics measures visits to this website. It sets cookies in your browser and receives your IP address and browser details. You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s opt-out add-on.
  • Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this website. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address when it does.

Like most online services, these providers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and browser details, to keep their services running securely. Some of them store data outside Australia, including in the United States.

How we look after it

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and we delete it when we no longer need it.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Just get in touch using the form on our homepage and we’ll respond as soon as we can.

Concerns and complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. If you’re still not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
